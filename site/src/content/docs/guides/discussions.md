---
title: Discuss references and Projects
description: Choose a conversation space, post a message, exchange ideas, and remove messages when needed.
---

Use Discussions for reading questions, method comparisons, and reading-group plans. Choose the
location that matches the conversation:

| What you want to discuss | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| One reference's content, method, or conclusions | Open the Item's **Discussion** section |
| Reading-group plans or comparisons across references | Open the Project's **Discussion** area |
| A specific mark on a PDF page | Open that revision and use an [Annotation Reply](/guides/pdf-annotations/) |

## Post a message about a reference

1. Open the Item from Library and choose **Discussion**.
2. Write in **Add message**, for example, “Compare the sample split in Table 2 with our current dataset.”
3. Choose **Post message**.
4. Check the content, author, and posting time in the message list.

Colleagues can open this Item's Discussion to read and continue the conversation. Refer to an
earlier author's point or a specific question to make the exchange easy to follow.

## Plan shared reading in a Project

1. Open **Projects** and choose the Project you are working in.
2. Find **Discussion** and write in **Write a message**.
3. Choose **Post** and check the posted message in the list.

For example, share the next reading's references, time, and questions. Colleagues can continue
the conversation in the same Project. See [Project collaboration](/guides/collaboration/) for
invitations, participation, and shared Annotations.

## Delete or moderate a message

Use **Delete** beside your own message. Deletion permanently removes the message; copy its text
first if you want to keep it.

Workspace owners and admins can use **Moderate** for another person's message:

1. Choose **Moderate** beside the message.
2. Enter a **Moderation reason**, such as “Duplicate content, incorporated into the reading summary.”
3. Choose **Remove message** and check that the list updates.

The reason is recorded in the audit log. See [member management](/administration/workspaces-and-members/)
for role assignments.
