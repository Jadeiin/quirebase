title: Start your research library
description: Sign in, choose a Workspace, import your first reference, and open a PDF.
---

If your team already runs Quirebase, ask for its application URL and an account or Invitation.
To run your own installation, start with [deployment](/administration/deployment/).

## Choose a Workspace

Sign in and open **My workspaces** or use the Workspace menu to select a Workspace.
New Users receive an ordinary Workspace when their account is provisioned. To work with an existing
team, accept a separate Workspace Invitation using your existing account.

Your selected Workspace determines the Items, Tags, Projects, and actions available to you.
If you cannot import, ask a Workspace governor for the appropriate role.

## Import a reference

1. Open **Import** and choose **Identifiers**.
2. Paste a DOI or supported identifier, one per line. For example, `10.1073/pnas.92.6.2288`.
3. Leave the source on **Auto detect** and prepare the preview.
4. Review the Candidate Records and diagnostics, then confirm your selection.
5. Open the imported Item in **Library** and check its metadata.

Import preparation does not create Items. A reference enters your Library only after confirmation.
You can also [import a bibliography or PDFs](/guides/import/).

## Add and read a PDF

Open the Item's **Files** section, upload a primary PDF, and wait for processing to finish.
Each primary PDF is a separate File Revision. Once it is ready, open it in the PDF reader.

Choose **Annotations → Save new annotations to → Private annotations** for personal notes.
To share marks with collaborators, first add the Item to a Project, then choose that Project as the
annotation destination. See [PDF annotations](/guides/pdf-annotations/) for saving and export.

## Organize and share

Use Tags for topics across your Workspace and Projects for a collaborative working set.
An Item can belong to several Projects in the same Workspace without being duplicated.

Before inviting collaborators, read [Workspaces and Projects](/concepts/workspaces-and-projects/).
Project participation does not grant additional access to the canonical Workspace Item.

When your first reading workflow is complete, set up
[citation and export defaults](/guides/citations-and-exports/) and review your
[account credentials](/guides/account/).
